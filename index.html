Keeping Land in the Family?

Tracing kinship, accumulation, and division across Chinese land deeds from Fujian, 1875–1936

族契
01 · Question

Did land stay within kin networks?

How did kinship shape the transfer, consolidation, and division of land in a small set of Chinese land deeds?

Large statistics can tell us how often relatives traded with one another. They cannot tell us how property moved between them. So we use the whole collection only as background, and follow three families through runs of linked documents: deeds, pawn contracts, tax receipts, and a household division agreement.

The question mark in our title is on purpose. Some land did move along kin lines. But outsiders kept entering, pawned land could be redeemed, and brothers divided what one generation had gathered.

03 · Method

How we identify kinship

Not all evidence of kinship is equally strong. We ranked it in three levels and only make claims from the top one.

23records

Explicit kinship term strongest

The deed itself names the relationship between seller and buyer: 族兄、族叔、堂兄、從弟、宗兄、侄、叔祖. This is about one record in ten. It is a lower bound: we counted only the terms our notes recorded, and we left out cases where the relatives were only co-sellers on the same side.

39of 122

Same surname suggestive

Of the 122 records whose catalog entry names both a seller and a buyer, 39 (32%) share a surname. Sharing a surname makes kinship more likely, but it does not prove it.

✓where possible

Metadata checked against the text verification

Catalog titles give "Seller" and "Buyer", and these are sometimes wrong. We trust the wording of the document over the catalog, and we mark every disagreement instead of fixing it silently.

Reading direction: in 林應香 賣與族兄林萬開, the seller calls the buyer "clan elder brother". So 族兄 is the buyer's relation to the seller.

CASE 1

林萬開 LIN Wankai

kin-based acquisition → accumulation → household division → continued outside acquisition

  1. 1875
    賣斷根面契outright sale of full rights (根 + 面)
    林應香 → 族兄林萬開
    田價錢壹百零捌千文108,000 cash草市都馬坑pitt:…607785
    Re-registered in 1913: 契單, 田 2.3 畝, 申銀 108 元 · pitt:…607694
  2. 1881
    賣斷田根契outright sale of subsoil rights (田根)
    林春發 → 族叔林萬開
    田根價錢壹百壹拾千文110,000 cash · 4 plots + a pond, 2.5 畝pitt:…607801
  3. 1884
    撥典田面契transfer of a pawned surface right (田面)
    林秋溥 → 堂兄林萬開
    田面價鏡銀肆拾肆兩44 taels · 4.8 畝 · rent 860 斤 of grain a yearpitt:…607751
    Not a purchase. 林秋溥 had taken this land in pawn from an outsider, 何德興, and passed the pawn on to his cousin. The original owner could still redeem it (照契面銀兩取贖). Land that "entered the family" here came from outside, and could leave again.
  4. 1887
    契尾official tax receipt
    業戶林萬開 買 林秋溥田
    44 taels, 3% tax · 侯字壹萬叁千拾陸號pitt:…607876
    Possible metadata reversal. The catalog lists 林萬開 as seller. The receipt names him as the buyer. See Errors.
  5. 1891
    分關書household division agreement · 10 pages
    林萬開 與 兄弟萬拱、萬欽 · 分作三房
    Fields, houses, hill land, and gardens split into three branches; grain tax and 350 斤 of rent assignedpitt:…607645
  6. 1894
    斷賣契單1913 registration of an 1894 outright sale
    王兆熙 → 林萬開 (witness 王聯登)
    田 2 畝 · 申銀 138 元 · 三十八都白龍坛pitt:…607744
    Catalog says the seller is 王聯鄧. Our notes on the document read 王聯登 as the witness, not the seller.

Takeaway. In about ten years 林萬開 gathered land from three relatives: a clan younger brother, a clan nephew, and a cousin. Then he and his brothers divided the family estate, and three years after that he was buying from the 王 family. The family estate was a stage in a longer process, not its end point.

05 · Finding

What the deeds show

Kinship did not simply keep land unchanged within a family. Property moved through kin networks by repeated purchase, by pawning followed by outright sale, and by partition among brothers.

Repeated purchase

林萬開 and 陳欽瑞 each gathered plots from several relatives. The relationship is written into the deed.

Pawn, then sale

陳必清's house passed to 陳必泉 in two steps, and the tax receipt adds the two prices together.

Partition

The 1891 division agreement split 林萬開's family estate into three branches.

Outsiders keep entering

The 何 family (behind the 1884 pawn) and the 王 family (1894) show that kin networks were open to outsiders.

Family property was not static. It was constantly rearranged through deals among relatives, and between relatives and outsiders.

Scope: three families, sixteen records, all from Fujian. The pattern illustrates mechanisms and is not a rate for the whole collection.

07 · Method disclosure & credits

How this was made

Tools and models

  • Claude (Anthropic), in Claude Desktop, read the deeds with us and extracted each record into a table: date, type, parties, place, land, price, and a confidence level.
  • Claude (Opus 5.5) in Claude Code helped search the table, count the kinship evidence, and build this website.
  • The data is in this repository as CSV and XLSX.

What we checked ourselves

  • Kinship terms are counted only where they were recorded from the document text.
  • Each record carries a confidence level (93 high, 120 medium, 21 low).
  • Disagreements between the catalog and the document are marked, not silently fixed.
  • No document images are reproduced here. Every record links to the library's own page.
